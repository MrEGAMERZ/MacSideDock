# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in SIDEDOCK a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and specific in code review and issues
- Focusing on what would make the Dock feel better on a real Mac
- Accepting that “no” to widgets, previews, or extra permissions is a product decision, not a personal slight
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report incidents by opening a GitHub issue or contacting the maintainer via GitHub ([@MrEGAMERZ](https://github.com/MrEGAMERZ)). The project team will review and respond as soon as possible.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
